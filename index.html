05 / RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

PRINT. TEST.
REFINE. RELEASE.

Every R3D design is physically printed, assembled and meticulously tested before release. Fit, strength, mechanical function and RC integration are checked through repeated prototypes and real-world testing, with revisions made until the design is ready to build and use.

01 / PRINT

Prototype

Parts are physically printed to verify tolerances, assembly and real-world printability.

02 / TEST

Evaluate

Mechanical movement, fit, strength and RC integration are tested under practical conditions.

03 / REFINE

Iterate

Geometry and mechanisms are revised wherever testing identifies an opportunity to improve.

04 / RELEASE

Validated

The final files are released only after the design has completed the print-and-test cycle.
