R3D MARINE — ENGINEERING MEDIA FOLDER

Upload the two engineering cross-section images into this folder.
The website detects these filenames automatically:

cross-section-01.png
cross-section-02.png

You may also use:
.jpg
.jpeg
.webp

Example:
cross-section-01.jpg
cross-section-02.webp

The engineering section will stay clean if the files are missing. As soon as one or both images are uploaded and Cloudflare redeploys, the CROSS SECTIONS panel appears automatically.

Recommended image preparation:
- landscape or square technical render
- dark or neutral background works best
- 1600–2400 px wide is enough
- export cleanly from CAD/Fusion 360 with internal geometry clearly visible
